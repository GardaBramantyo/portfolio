Burning questions

Can I pause or skip a delivery?+

Anytime, from the link in every shipping email. No login, no phone call, no guilt trip.

Whole beans or ground?+

Your call. We grind for espresso, moka pot, filter, French press or cold brew. Whole beans stay fresh longest.

How far do you deliver?+

Free bike delivery within five miles of the roastery. Everywhere else in the country ships tracked for $4, free on the Regular plan.

Are the bags compostable?+

Yes, home-compostable, valve included. Or drop empties at the café and we’ll handle them.

Your first bag is half price.

Drop your email and we’ll send a code for 50% off your first subscription bag. Roasting starts Tuesday.

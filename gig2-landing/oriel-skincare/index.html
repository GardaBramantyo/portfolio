Why it exists

Over-exfoliated, over-treated and over it? Your skin doesn’t need another active. It needs a break.

Most routines strip the barrier, the thin layer of lipids that keeps water in and irritants out. When it breaks down, everything stings, flakes or flushes.

No. 3 does one job well. It restores the lipids your skin is missing, calms visible redness and gets out of the way, so the rest of your routine can work again.

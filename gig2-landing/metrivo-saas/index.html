FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Book a 20-minute walkthrough.

How long does setup take?+

Most teams see their first reconciled MRR bridge in under 15 minutes. Historical backfill of up to five years runs in the background and usually finishes the same day.

Does Metrivo replace our BI tool?+

No. It handles the revenue model your BI tool struggles with. You can sync every Metrivo metric back to your warehouse and keep building custom reports there.

Is our data secure?+

Connections are read-only, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you choose US or EU hosting. We never train models on customer data.

What happens after the trial?+

You pick a plan or your workspace pauses. Nothing is billed automatically, and paused workspaces are kept for 30 days.
